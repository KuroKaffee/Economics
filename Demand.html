Demand

Demand refers to the quantity of a good or service that consumers are willing and able to purchase at various prices during a given period.

The law of demand states that, all else being equal, as the price of a good or service increases, the quantity demanded decreases, and vice versa.

This means that there is an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded.

Which then influences supply as well

Demand Graph

Image by CFI

Demand graphs typically show a downward slope, showing the inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded.

As the price of a good or service increases, consumers are less willing to purchase it, leading to a decrease in quantity demanded.

On the other hand, as the price decreases, consumers are more willing to purchase the good or service.

Shifters

Image by Pexels

Shifters are factors that can cause the Demand curve to shift.

Shifters in Demand include Workers income,Value on the goods,or expected trends.

Personal Taste also plays a role in shifting this graph, as people may just not want that good because of the season or that they just dont like it
