The Overview of Supply and Demand

Supply and demand are fundamental concepts in economics that determine the price and quantity of goods and services in a market.

When supply exceeds demand, prices tend to fall, and when demand exceeds supply, prices tend to rise.

Both Supply and Demand compliment eachother. Supply determines the demand, and the demand determines the supply.

This System ensures the balance of the market in society.
