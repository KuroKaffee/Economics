Prices

Prices are the amounts of money that buyers pay to sellers for goods or services.

Prices play a crucial role in the allocation of resources in a market economy.

They help determine the quantity of a good or service that consumers are willing to purchase and the quantity that producers are willing to supply.

When prices are high, consumers may buy less, and when prices are low, consumers may buy more.

Equilibrium

Equilibrium price is the price at which the quantity of a good or service demanded by consumers equals the quantity supplied by producers.

At this price, there is no shortage or surplus of the good or service, and the market is balanced.

Equilibrium is vital for companies to determine in order to find the golden zone for sales

Being above or below this quantity by a good amount will result in either a shortage or surplus.
