Equilibrium

Equilibrium refers to the point at which the quantity of a good or service that consumers are willing and able to purchase equals the quantity that producers are willing and able to supply.

At this point, the market is in balance, and there is no tendency for the price to change.

The equilibrium price is the price at which the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied.

The equilibrium quantity is the quantity at which the quantity demanded equals the quantity supplied.
